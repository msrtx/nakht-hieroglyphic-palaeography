Stroke Order

Egyptian hieroglyphic writing follows specific conventions for stroke execution and sign construction. Scribes were trained to produce consistent forms using particular brush or reed pen techniques. Observations from this manuscript indicate careful execution with attention to proportional balance within each sign.
