Introduction

This digital edition presents BMI EA 10473,1, a beautifully preserved Book of the Dead belonging to Nakht, royal scribe and military commander of the Late Eighteenth Dynasty (~1350 BCE).

Through this site, you can explore:

  • Historical context — Dating, provenance, and physical description
  • Palaeographic analysis — Hieroglyphic signs and colour usage
  • Cultural significance — Scribes, artists, and script traditions
